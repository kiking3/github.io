PLAYER WELLBEING

Play with perspective.

Before playing, decide how much time and money you are comfortable spending. Stick to those limits and never borrow money to play.

  • Take regular breaks and avoid chasing losses.
  • Keep gaming separate from work, bills, and essential spending.
  • Do not play when distressed or under the influence.
  • If it is becoming difficult to stop, step away and seek independent support.
